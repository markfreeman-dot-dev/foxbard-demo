# Foxbard design changelog

## 0.1.0 · 2026-11-11 (T1118)
- First system. Cloned from the Foxrun framework (tokens first, DESIGN.md generated by tools/build.py).
- Look from Mark Freeman's Look Lab ballot (decision g-foxbard-look-is-marks-ballot): Archivo wide caps wordmark, ink and lime, Supreme headlines, Hanken Grotesk body, marks plus one takeaway bar, soft outlined cards.
- Three themes: dark (default for decks and site), white, paper (default for documents). Lime turns into a highlighter on light themes; olive draws thin marks there.
- components/foxbard.css and components/theme-toggle.js shared by the deck and the site.
- Rule call after the independent read: the takeaway bar is the only lime block; on white and paper the headline highlighter counts as a mark.
- Deck: Chris's v24.0, 33 pages word for word, 3 themes, PDFs. Site: foxbard.com redesign with Hero A/B.

## 0.2.0 · 2026-11-11 (T1118, round 2)
- Four palettes (current, lime-ink default, lemon, mint) after the lime research (Robinhood #CCFF00 overlap).
- Legibility from Marcus's Clipy review: bigger and darker labels, stronger type ramp, framed cards and panels, logo chips and headshots from Chris's PDF, charts per the dataviz skill.

## 0.3.0 · 2026-11-11 (T1118, locked)
- Color locked: lime-ink is the only palette; the other three are removed. Support color is blue (decision g-foxbard-support-blue); coral, violet and copper stay as samples only.
- Paper is linen #F8F5EF. Light-theme chart bars are lime with an ink outline; context gray #8A8A86.
- Fewer boxes: open blocks (top rule only) where a frame adds nothing. One support-color item per card; field labels in muted gray.
- Deck: p3 always-on agents (Duet, Muse, Dots, Grok Bot) and p14 St. Paul's, both ruled by Marcus. Site: carried onto the locked system.
