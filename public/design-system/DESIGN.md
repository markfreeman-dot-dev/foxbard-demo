# Foxbard design, v0.3.0

The file every agent reads first for Foxbard. Exact values live in `tokens/tokens.json` (the source); the token section below is generated from it by `tools/build.py`. Rules are written by hand.

The look is ruled by decision `g-foxbard-look-is-marks-ballot` (Mark Freeman's ballot, 11.11): Archivo wide caps wordmark, ink and lime, Supreme headlines, Hanken Grotesk body, marks plus one takeaway bar, soft corners with outlined cards.

## Feel
AI that pays attention. Ink carries the page. Lime works like a highlighter: it marks the one thing to notice, and nothing else. Calm, exact, and sure of itself, for family offices and the people who back them.

## Themes
Three themes share every rule. Each page or file offers a toggle.
- **Dark** (default for decks and the site): ink `#0B0B0C`. Lime marks read at full strength.
- **White:** pure `#FFFFFF`.
- **Paper** (default for documents and the data room): warm paper `#FAFAF7`, off-white. Never cream: cream with a serif and a clay accent is another brand's look.
- On white and paper, lime turns into a highlighter: a lime band behind ink text. Thin marks (dashes, edges) use ink, because lime is too pale for thin lines on light grounds.

## Logo
- Mark: the angular fox head from foxbard.com, solid, one color (text color of the theme). `assets/logo/mark-white.svg`. It is a trace of a picture, not a drawn vector: redraw before print or large use.
- Wordmark: FOXBARD in Archivo, caps, weight 800, width 125%, tracking 0.06em, with the mark to its left. Until a drawn vector exists, set it as live type with `.fb-wordmark` in `components/foxbard.css`.
- Never color the wordmark lime. Never add a gradient, glow, or shadow.

## Rules
- **Ink first.** Text, buttons, and outlines use the theme's text color. Buttons are text-color fills with background-color text, or an outline. Never a lime button; one lime block per page at most (the takeaway bar).
- **One accent, used as a highlighter.** Lime marks: the eyebrow dash, the second clause of a headline, the active card's edge, tags, the one chart series the page is about, and one takeaway bar per page. If you can count more than five lime marks on a page, take some away.
- **The takeaway bar** is the only lime block: one per page, ink text on lime, the sentence the reader must keep. On white and paper the headline highlighter is the light-theme form of the accent clause (lime text on dark) and counts as a mark, not a fill.
- **Cards** are outlined (1px line, 6px corners), never filled with gray. The active or concluding card gets the accent edge.
- **Headlines:** Supreme 700, normal tracking, sentence case. The second clause may carry the accent (lime on dark, highlighter on light).
- **Labels:** Geist Mono caps with 0.06em tracking for eyebrows, tags, page numbers, and table figures.
- **Body:** Hanken Grotesk 400; bold lead-ins at 600, never whole paragraphs.
- **Status colors** mean status only (success, warning, error, info), always with a word or icon. Never use them as decoration.
- **Charts:** the series the slide is about is `chart-1` (lime); the baseline is `chart-2` (gray). Draw to scale from the source numbers; label values directly.
- **Icons:** line icons only (Hugeicons). Never emoji.
- **Contrast:** every text pair passes 4.5:1 in all three themes (see the check above).
- **Content is the author's.** Restyling a Foxbard document never changes its words, numbers, or footnotes.

## Color (v0.3.0, locked 11.11)
- **One palette: lime-ink.** Lime is the highlighter, the takeaway bar and chart series 1; thin marks on white and paper are ink. Set `data-palette="lime-ink"` on `<html>`.
- **Support color: blue** (`#2459E6` on white and paper, `#7FA2FF` on dark), set with `data-support="blue"`. It is the only support color.
- **One support item per card.** Use it for the one line that names the card (a role, a tag) or for arrows and chart series 3. Never for every label.
- **Paper is linen** `#F8F5EF`. **Chart bars** on white and paper are lime with a 1px ink outline; the context series is gray `#8A8A86`.

## Legibility rules (round 2, from Marcus's review 11.11)
- **Labels:** page eyebrows are full ink. Field labels inside a card (SCHOOLS, TRACK RECORD) are muted gray caps so the one support-color line stands out.
- **A clear ramp:** slide headline, then card title (Supreme), then bold counts and figures (`.fb-count`), then body (`--fb-text-2`), then labels.
- **Frame the key block.** Cards sit on `--fb-surface` with a hairline; the one block a page is about is a `.fb-panel`.
- **No empty card bottoms.** Size type so content fills the card; dense pages use `.is-dense`, never smaller than about 1.1% of slide width.
- **Logos and faces:** `.fb-chiplogo` chips on a white ground in every theme; `.fb-face` round headshots. Use the brand's own files, never redraw a logo.
- **Charts follow the dataviz skill:** bars at most 24px thick with a 4px rounded end, a 2px gap between touching segments, a label inside a segment only when it fits (the legend names the rest), a legend for two or more series, values labeled directly. Run the validator on the identity colors.

## Deck recipe (16:9)
1. One left margin; the eyebrow (mono caps with a lime dash) at the top left, the wordmark at the top right.
2. Headline in Supreme; the second clause in the accent.
3. The body is one composition: a row of 3 to 5 numbered cards joined by arrows, a big number with a chart, or a table.
4. One takeaway bar under the body.
5. The footnote and the page number on one line at the bottom, mono, muted.
6. Check the three themes, then the page at phone width.

## Never
- A navy-to-blue gradient, glow, or orbs. The generic AI look.
- Navy with mid blue (the crowded AI-services lane).
- Cream paper with a serif and a clay accent.
- Lime text on white or paper. Lime as a page background.
- Gray fills behind content. Emoji. Two text faces in one paragraph.
- Changing the author's words while restyling.
